01 / where I am right now

A little about me.

I’m a student, a curious learner, and someone who likes turning ideas into things I can try.

“A new problem is a chance to learn something I couldn’t do yesterday.”

I’m Devendra Kumar Reddy, studying Computer Science and Engineering at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering. I enjoy learning a concept and then finding a way to put it to work.

That’s what I liked about making a car rental app: I could see how the pieces came together into something someone might actually use. With FinGaze, I got to explore a very different question — how AI could help make sense of risk in the stock market. Both projects left me with more questions, which is usually a good sign.

I’m still figuring out exactly where I want to go. For now, I’m happy to keep learning, building, and getting a little better with each attempt.

FinGaze

I wanted to explore how AI could help make sense of market risk. I built a small trading environment, experimented with sentiment-aware signals, and compared different agents against historical data. It taught me that a promising idea still needs careful testing.

Python · FinRL · Deep Reinforcement Learning
Web Application2025

Car Rental Management

I wanted vehicle browsing and booking to feel straightforward. I built a responsive app where people can explore cars, see rental details, and manage bookings. Connecting the React interface to Firebase helped me understand how the pieces of a useful web app fit together.
